## Problem

Faded, blurred, scratched family photos, and no simple way for a non-technical person to bring them back.

## Approach

- Native iPhone app: upload a photo, get a denoised, repaired, and colorized version with a before/after comparison.
- Restoration runs on a cloud backend and results sync to a personal gallery.

## Result

A working product loop from camera roll to restored image, used first on my own family's photos.
